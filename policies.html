Merci Policies

Please read our policies before booking your appointment. By booking an appointment, you agree to follow these policies.

Cancellation Policy

Appointments can be cancelled 3 days or more before the scheduled appointment. Cancellations made less than 3 days before the appointment are not allowed.

Late Arrivals

A grace period of 10–15 minutes is allowed. Arriving more than 15 minutes late may result in your appointment being considered cancelled.

No-Show Policy

If you do not show up for your appointment, the appointment will be cancelled. Repeated no-shows or unreasonable excuses may result in you being unable to book future appointments.

Refund Policy

No refunds are offered. Please make sure you are comfortable with your chosen service before your appointment.

Deposits

Deposits are not currently required. This policy may be updated in the future.

Thank you for respecting Merci's policies 💗
